MEET BULLET PRADEEP.

THE MAN BEHIND CLASSIC MOTORS.

A PASSION THAT BECAME A LIFE.

Pradeep N, popularly known as , was born in 1974. Long before motorcycles became his profession, the machines had already caught his imagination.

In 1988, when he was 14, he began spending time around his elder brother Prasad’s workshop — learning through observation, listening closely and slowly understanding the craft behind the machine.

What began as passion and close exposure grew into years of practical knowledge. After Prasad’s passing, Pradeep took on the responsibility of carrying the workshop forward — turning experience into the foundation for what would become Classic Motors.

From 2002 to 2010, that foundation travelled beyond the workshop. Pradeep worked as a rally mechanic alongside foreign riders and experienced demanding roads and riding environments across Himachal Pradesh, Goa and Gokarna.
