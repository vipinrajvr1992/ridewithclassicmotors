THE STORY OF BULLET PRADEEP

Pradeep N, popularly known as "Bullet Pradeep", entered the motorcycle mechanic field at the age of 18.

His journey with Royal Enfield began in 1988, inspired greatly by his elder brother Prasad.

Over the years, motorcycles became more than a profession. They became a way of life.

His passion for Enfield runs so deep that the word "Enfield" is engraved on his wedding ring — a small personal detail that reflects a lifetime connected with motorcycles.
