SERVICE FAQ

BEFORE YOU
BOOK.

Quick answers about Classic Motors workshop services.

Do I need an appointment?

It is best to send a service enquiry or contact Classic Motors before visiting so the workshop can discuss your requirement and availability.

What motorcycles do you service?

Classic Motors is positioned as an independent Royal Enfield specialist workshop. Contact the workshop with your motorcycle details before booking.

Can you prepare a motorcycle for a long ride?

Yes, long-ride and touring preparation is one of the workshop service categories. Share your planned requirement and preferred visit date.

Can I ask for restoration work?

Custom restoration is offered as a service category. The motorcycle and the required scope should be discussed directly with Classic Motors.
