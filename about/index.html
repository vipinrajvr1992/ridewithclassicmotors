A passion that found its place

BEFORE THE WORKSHOP, THERE WAS THE PASSION.

Pradeep N was born in 1974. Long before Classic Motors became a name riders could visit, motorcycles had already become part of his world.

In 1988, when he was 14, that passion took him closer to the place where he could understand the machines he was drawn to. He began learning around his elder brother Prasad’s workshop — watching, listening, helping where he could and slowly understanding what it meant to work with a motorcycle.

It was not simply about learning a trade. It was the beginning of a relationship with machines that stayed with him for years. The passion came first. The workshop gave it direction.

That early connection with Royal Enfield became a defining part of his journey — one that would eventually lead from workshop floors to rally roads and, later, to the responsibility of carrying the workshop forward.

THE PERSON WHO OPENED THE DOOR

FROM PRASAD, A WAY FORWARD.

Pradeep’s elder brother Prasad was an important part of those early years. His workshop became the environment where Pradeep could stay close to motorcycles, observe the work and learn from real machines rather than from theory alone.

That connection shaped more than mechanical understanding. It gave Pradeep a place where his natural passion for motorcycles could grow into discipline, experience and confidence.

After Prasad’s passing, Pradeep took on the responsibility of carrying the workshop forward. What began beside his brother became something he had to stand behind himself.

That is part of what makes Classic Motors personal: it is not only a workshop — it is a continuation of a journey that started with family.

ABOUT BULLET PRADEEP.

A few direct answers for riders who want to know the person behind Classic Motors.

Who is Bullet Pradeep?

Bullet Pradeep is the name associated with Pradeep N, the motorcycle specialist behind Classic Motors in Mananthavady, Wayanad.

When did Pradeep’s motorcycle journey begin?

Pradeep was born in 1974. In 1988, at the age of 14, he began learning around his elder brother Prasad’s workshop. That early exposure became the beginning of his long hands-on motorcycle journey.

Was Pradeep working as a mechanic at 14?

The story is better understood as early workshop exposure and learning: he was drawn to motorcycles and began learning around Prasad’s workshop. His experience and responsibility developed over the years that followed.

How did Prasad influence the journey?

Prasad, Pradeep’s elder brother, provided the workshop environment where Pradeep could stay close to motorcycles and learn through practical experience. After Prasad’s passing, Pradeep took on the responsibility of carrying the workshop forward.

Does Bullet Pradeep have rally experience?

Yes. Pradeep worked as a rally mechanic from 2002 to 2010, including experience with foreign riders and motorcycle events and journeys in Himachal Pradesh, Goa and Gokarna.

Is Classic Motors an official Royal Enfield service centre?

No. Classic Motors is an independent motorcycle workshop and is not an official Royal Enfield authorised service centre.
