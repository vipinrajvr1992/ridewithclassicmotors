THE STORY BEHIND CLASSIC MOTORS

THE LEGEND OF BULLET PRADEEP

A lifetime built around motorcycles, machines, roads and the Royal Enfield spirit.

MORE THAN A WORKSHOP

Some mechanics repair motorcycles.
Some understand them.

Classic Motors is built around a simple idea: a motorcycle should be understood before it is worked on.

At the heart of Classic Motors is Pradeep N, widely known among riders as Bullet Pradeep. His journey with motorcycles began when he was just 18 years old.

What started as a passion for machines gradually became a lifetime of hands-on experience, particularly with Royal Enfield motorcycles.

His association with Royal Enfield dates back to 1988 — a connection that has continued through changing generations of motorcycles, riding styles and technology.

PRASAD — ELDER BROTHER
THE INSPIRATION

FROM FAMILY, A PASSION.

Pradeep's elder brother, Prasad, was an important influence during the early part of his journey.

That early exposure to motorcycles helped shape a practical understanding of machines — not just how they work, but how they behave on the road.

Over the years, that knowledge became experience, and experience became the foundation on which Classic Motors was built.

RALLY EXPERIENCE

BUILT FOR THE LONG ROAD.

Between 2002 and 2010, Pradeep worked as a rally mechanic, gaining experience around demanding rides, challenging terrain and riders who depended on their machines.

His rally experience included working with foreign riders and being involved in motorcycle events and journeys across locations including Himachal Pradesh, Goa and Gokarna.

That experience created a different understanding of motorcycle preparation — one where reliability, mechanical condition and attention to detail matter before a rider leaves the workshop.
