PRIVACY POLICY

How Classic Motors handles information submitted through this website.

CLASSIC MOTORS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Classic Motors respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, why it may be used, and how you can contact us about it.

01

Information We May Collect

When you contact Classic Motors or submit a service enquiry through this website, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Email address, if provided
  • Motorcycle make, model and year
  • Type of service or enquiry
  • Preferred service date or time
  • Additional information included in your message

You are not required to provide information that is not necessary for your particular enquiry.

02

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to Classic Motors may be used to:

  • Respond to service and contact enquiries
  • Discuss motorcycle service requirements
  • Coordinate appointments or workshop visits
  • Respond to Rider's Den booking enquiries
  • Communicate regarding an enquiry you initiated
  • Maintain necessary enquiry or service records
  • Help improve the website and customer experience
  • Protect the website and its users from misuse
03

WhatsApp Enquiries

Some forms on this website prepare a WhatsApp message containing the information you entered. When you choose to send that message, the information is transferred to WhatsApp through your device or browser.

WhatsApp is an independent third-party service. Its own terms and privacy practices apply to information processed through its platform.

WhatsApp Privacy Information
04

Website Hosting & Technical Information

Like many websites, the hosting or server infrastructure used for this website may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, device information, access times and server logs.

Such information may be used by the hosting infrastructure for security, reliability, troubleshooting and normal operation of the website.

05

Cookies & Tracking

This website does not intentionally use tracking cookies for advertising or behavioural profiling at present.

However, third-party services or hosting infrastructure linked from or supporting the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their respective privacy policies apply to those services.

06

Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to external websites and services, including:

  • WhatsApp
  • Instagram
  • Google Maps / Google services
  • MakeMyTrip
  • Agoda
  • Booking.com

These services are operated independently from Classic Motors. Once you leave this website, the privacy practices of the external service apply.

Classic Motors does not control the privacy policies, cookies or data practices of third-party websites.

07

Data Sharing

Classic Motors does not sell or rent personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, process an enquiry, provide a requested service, maintain website security, or where required by applicable law.

Information submitted to third-party services directly by you, such as WhatsApp or booking platforms, is subject to the policies of those services.

08

How Long We Keep Information

Enquiry and service-related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, coordinate services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve issues, or meet applicable obligations.

We do not intend to retain personal information longer than reasonably necessary for these purposes.

09

Children's Privacy

This website is not specifically directed toward children. Classic Motors does not intentionally seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted by a child and requires attention, please contact us.

10

Your Choices & Requests

If you have questions about information you have submitted to Classic Motors, or would like to request clarification regarding its use, you can contact us directly.

Email classicmotorsre@gmail.com
11

Changes to This Policy

Classic Motors may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the website, services or information-handling practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Important Note

This Privacy Policy describes the current website's general information-handling practices. It is not intended to create contractual rights or replace professional legal advice.

PRIVACY FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers about information submitted through the Classic Motors website.

What information does Classic Motors collect?

Information you voluntarily provide through service or contact enquiries may include your name, phone number, email, motorcycle details, requested service, preferred date and your message.

Does Classic Motors sell my personal data?

Classic Motors does not sell or rent personal information submitted through this website.

Does the website use tracking cookies?

The website does not intentionally use tracking cookies for advertising or behavioural profiling at present.

What happens when I submit an enquiry through WhatsApp?

The website prepares a WhatsApp message using the information you entered. After you choose to send it, the information is processed through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

How can I contact Classic Motors about privacy?

You can contact Classic Motors at classicmotorsre@gmail.com for privacy-related questions regarding information submitted through this website.
