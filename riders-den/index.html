STAY CLOSE TO THE ROAD.

Rider's Den is a stay destination in Mananthavady, Wayanad, created with a clear connection to motorcycle culture.

The property features five individually named rooms inspired by Royal Enfield motorcycles: Classic, Himalayan, Hunter, Interceptor and Supermeteor.

Whether you are exploring Wayanad, travelling through Mananthavady or simply looking for a comfortable base for your next ride, Rider's Den gives you a place to stop, recharge and continue your journey.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Useful information before booking your stay.

Where is Rider's Den located?

Rider's Den is located in Mananthavady, Wayanad, Kerala, near the Valliyurkav Temple area.

How many rooms does Rider's Den have?

The Rider's Den room collection includes five room types: Classic, Himalayan, Hunter, Interceptor and Supermeteor.

Can I book Rider's Den directly?

Yes. Direct booking enquiries can be made by WhatsApp or phone at +91 94491 50965.

Yes. Rider's Den is listed on MakeMyTrip. Use the MakeMyTrip booking button on this page to check current availability and rates.

Are room prices fixed on this website?

Rates and availability can change depending on dates, occupancy and booking channel. Please check the current booking rate before confirming your stay.
